Over time: the most populated dot at each output, with the time bar, and the dots' shares at the seed, on the way and at the network's edge; dense outputs resolve the nucleation burst. In the acetate network the dots wait at the seed and cross to the edge faster than the outputs (under 3 % ever sit in between), so the burst is the shares moving and the growth of one dot is a growth path. Growth (pick one of the most populated at the time bar's time): from the pool to that state, the chain of steps along which the most material has moved so far, i.e. whose narrowest step carried the most (the net flux integrated over the run), played forward step by step. Dissolution (the populated dots losing more CdSe to smaller states than they gain, right then): from that dot down the steps with net flux to the smaller state at that time, the chain with the largest narrowest flux, back to the pool when the flux reaches it. Every frame on the scale of the largest, centred; drag to turn it, the turn is kept from frame to frame.

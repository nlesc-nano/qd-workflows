2 · Kinetic growth nucleation from Ω, monomer consumption, size-law growth, facet shape

Dot shape:
Growth control:
Uses the conditions on the left. Growth duration is how long the kinetics is integrated after injection; it is independent of the time you look at Ω above. Each dot grows or dissolves by dm/dt = A·NA(C − Ceq(m)) / (A/4πDr + 1/keff) (diffusion in series with the surface reaction), Ceq from the Gibbs–Thomson chemical potential of its shape. The surface reaction is computed from the facet incorporation rates of the NEB pilot, keff = Σf Afkf(1 − θf)/A over the dot's exposed facets. With the facet shape rule the growth is shared among the facets by their relative speeds ρfkf(1 − θf)[1 − e−(μs−μf)/kT]Pf (Pf: layer nucleation from the island step βf); otherwise every dot keeps the Wulff shape.

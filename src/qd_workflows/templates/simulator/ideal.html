Ideal growth against the full atomistic model

Two runs of the atomistic simulator at the same conditions. Both nucleate on the grid's Ω(m, k) (Becker–Döring flux, the same birth size and hand-over) and draw on the same precursor pool with the computed equilibria. They differ only in how the dots grow: the ideal run grows spheres of one surface energy γ, with no CdCl₂ shell and one surface reaction rate kr; the full run grows the facets with their CdCl₂ coverage, their surface energies and their NEB incorporation rates. The textbook CNT model stays available.
